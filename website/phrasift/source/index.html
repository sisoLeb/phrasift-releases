System requirements.

The bundled Fast speech model and local translation both run on the CPU. You can use the app with integrated graphics and zero dedicated VRAM.

Windows · x64

Windows 10 or 11, a 64-bit Intel or AMD CPU with SSE 4.1, and Microsoft Edge WebView2.

Mac · Apple Silicon

Apple M1 or newer and macOS 14 or later.

Tested CPU: Apple M1 on macOS 15, with 3 logical processors and 7 GiB of unified memory on a hosted runner.

Mac · Intel

64-bit Intel Mac with SSE 4.1 and macOS 13 or later.

Tested CPU: Intel Core i7-8700B on macOS 15, with 4 logical processors and 14 GiB RAM on a hosted runner.

Frequently asked
questions.

Does my footage leave my computer?

No. The Phrasift desktop app analyses and exports your recordings 100% locally on your computer. Activation and deactivation contact {{LICENSE_SERVICE}}. The app can also download an optional speech model. Translation uses the bundled local model. Feedback opens an email draft for you to review and send. Your recordings, transcripts and voice profiles are never uploaded.

How do I receive my download and license key after buying?

After payment, check the email address you used at checkout for your Phrasift license key and installer link. Your key activates up to 2 computers. To move it to another computer, deactivate an existing installation from the app’s license menu.

What is the difference between Standard (€49.99) and Pro (€99.99)?

Both are one-time purchases with no recurring subscription. Standard (€49.99) unlocks unlimited recording length, unwatermarked MP4 video exports, Premiere/Resolve XML and Final Cut Pro FCPXML timeline exports, SRT/WebVTT subtitle exports, local translated captions, video effects and personal voice memory. Pro (€99.99) includes everything in Standard plus automated Batch Queue processing, Watch Folders, Script Comparison, and Saved Workflow presets.

Can I try captions and translation before paying?

Yes. The trial supports recordings up to 10 minutes, all supported spoken and caption languages, caption creation, translation, text editing and preview. You can export an MP4 with burned captions and the trial watermark. Downloading SRT/WebVTT subtitle files, clean MP4s or editor timelines requires Standard or Pro.

What is your refund policy?

We offer a 14-day refund policy. If Phrasift cannot run on your supported Windows or macOS computer due to a technical issue we cannot resolve, or if your license key has not yet been activated, email Samir Allam at samirallam730@gmail.com within 14 days of purchase with your order receipt for a full refund.

Do I need a dedicated graphics card?

CPU mode works on Windows and Mac with integrated graphics. Windows NVIDIA acceleration requires a separately installed CUDA runtime. See the tested hardware for the measured configuration.
