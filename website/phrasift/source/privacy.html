5. Desktop App Privacy (Local Processing)

Recordings are analysed and exported locally on your computer. Saved projects, rendered previews and processing caches stay on your device. Voice memory stores numerical acoustic features on your computer rather than uploading your recordings to any server. Voice profiles can be reset or deleted inside the app at any time.

The Base speech recognition model is bundled inside the installer for offline processing. When you explicitly activate or deactivate a license key, the app contacts {{LICENSE_SERVICE}} using only the license key and a local installation identifier. The app may also retrieve public product information from the Phrasift website. Your recordings, transcripts and voice profiles are never transmitted.

When you send feedback or a bug report in the app, Cloudflare receives the message and, if you select the option, the app version and operating system. Brevo delivers that message to Phrasift support. The service uses a short-lived, hashed network identifier to limit abuse. It does not attach recordings, transcripts, voice profiles, or logs.
